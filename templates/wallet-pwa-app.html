Recovery Phrase Explained

Understanding your wallet recovery key

Definition
A seed phrase is a set of twelve words that contains all the information about your wallet, including your funds. It is a secret code used to access your assets.
Security
You must keep your seed phrase secret and safe. If someone gets your seed phrase, they will gain control over your accounts.
Recovery
Save it in a place where you can access it. If you lose it, not even Oneta can help you recover it. It is the only way to access your wallet if you get locked out of the app or get a new device.
