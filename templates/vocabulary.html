A – C

Addresses, blocks & consensus

📍

Address

A shareable identifier you give to receive payments. Derived from your public key — sharing it reveals nothing about your private key.

📐

bech32

The address format MoonBite uses. Starts with moon1… on mainnet, tmoon1… on testnet. Case-insensitive with built-in error detection.

⛓️

Block

A batch of transactions bundled together and permanently added to the chain by a miner who found the valid proof-of-work. MoonBite produces one roughly every 10 minutes.

🔗

Blockchain

The ordered, append-only ledger of every block ever mined. Each block includes the hash of the previous one, making the chain tamper-evident from the genesis block forward.

🏆

Block reward

The MBITE paid to the miner who successfully mines a block. MoonBite starts at 50 MBITE per block and halves every 330,000 blocks.

🤝

Consensus

The agreement among all nodes about which chain is valid. In MoonBite, the chain with the most accumulated proof-of-work is the consensus chain.

✅

Confirmation

A block mined on top of the one containing your transaction. More confirmations means it's increasingly expensive for anyone to reverse the transaction.

D – H

Difficulty, forks & hashing

🎯

Difficulty

A number that controls how hard it is to find a valid block hash. The network adjusts it periodically to keep blocks arriving every ~10 minutes regardless of total hashrate.

🌿

Fork

A divergence in the chain. A soft fork tightens the rules (old nodes still accept new blocks). A hard fork changes rules in a way that requires all nodes to upgrade.

🔢

Full node

A node that downloads every block since genesis and validates each one against the full ruleset. Full nodes are the enforcers of the protocol — they don't trust anyone else's word.

📉

Halving

A scheduled event where the block reward is cut in half. MoonBite halves every 330,000 blocks (~6.27 years). This limits the total supply to ~33,000,000 MBITE.

#️⃣

Hash

The fixed-length output of a one-way cryptographic function. Blocks are identified by their hash. Changing even one byte of a block produces a completely different hash.

📊

Hashrate

How many hash computations a miner (or the whole network) performs per second. Higher total hashrate means more security — attacking the chain costs more work.

M – N

Mining, mempool & nodes

🧾

Mempool

The waiting room where unconfirmed transactions sit before a miner picks them up and includes them in a block. A full mempool usually means higher fee competition.

⛏️

Mining

The process of repeatedly hashing a block header (varying the nonce) until the hash meets the current difficulty target. The miner who succeeds adds the block and earns the reward.

🖧

Node

Any computer running MoonBite software and connected to the peer-to-peer network. Nodes share blocks and transactions, each independently validating what they receive.

🔢

Nonce

"Number used once." A field in each block header that miners increment (or randomise) with every hash attempt, looking for a result that meets the difficulty target.

🚫

No premine

MoonBite has no premine — the developers did not create coins for themselves before the public could mine. Every MBITE is earned through the same open proof-of-work process.

P – R

Proof-of-work, keys & regtest

🔑

Private key

A secret 256-bit number that authorises spending from your address. Anyone who has it can move your coins. Never share it — not even with this website.

💪

Proof-of-work

The mechanism that secures MoonBite. To add a block, a miner must demonstrate they performed real computational work — making it expensive to rewrite history.

🔐

Public key

Derived from the private key via an irreversible elliptic-curve operation. Your address is a hash of the public key. Sharing an address or public key is safe.

🔬

Regtest

Regression-test mode. A private, local network where blocks can be mined instantly. Used for development and testing — coins have no value and are not on a shared network.

S – W

RandomX, self-custody & wallets

🧠

RandomX

The proof-of-work algorithm used by MoonBite (and Monero). It runs randomly generated programs in memory, so an ordinary CPU is the best mining hardware — ASICs and GPU farms get no meaningful edge, and mining can't be monopolised.

🌱

Seed phrase

A list of 12 or 24 words that encodes your private key in a human-readable backup. Anyone who has your seed phrase controls your coins. Store it offline and never type it online.

🏠

Self-custody

Holding your own keys rather than leaving coins in an exchange or custodial service. The MoonBite wallet is non-custodial — your keys stay on your device.

🏝️

Testnet

A public test network that runs in parallel with mainnet. Coins have no market value. MoonBite is currently pre-mainnet — everything runs on testnet today.

📦

UTXO

Unspent Transaction Output. Your balance is not a single number — it's a collection of coin parcels (UTXOs) you have received and not yet spent, each with its own amount and locking script.

👛

Wallet

Software that manages your keys, tracks your UTXOs, and constructs transactions. The MoonBite web wallet is non-custodial — keys are generated and held locally by you.
