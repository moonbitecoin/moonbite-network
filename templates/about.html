About

Built in the open.

MoonBite is a fair-launch cryptocurrency with a simple promise: capped supply, no premine, and tools anyone can run. Here's where it stands and where it's going.

Mission

Fair money, verifiable by anyone

MoonBite exists to be sound, open and self-custodial from day one. Every MBITE is mined under one public schedule — no insiders, no hidden allocations, no trust required.
