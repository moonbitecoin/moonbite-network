About

No boardroom. No back room.

MoonBite is a fair-launch cryptocurrency built entirely in public: capped supply, zero premine, and every tool open for anyone to run. Here's exactly where it stands, and where it's headed — nothing hidden.

Mission

Sound money you don't have to trust.

MoonBite is meant to be open and self-custodial from block zero. Every MBITE is mined under one public schedule — no insiders, no hidden allocations, no one you have to take on faith.

Tokenomics

The whole supply, on one page.

Fixed emission, hard cap, no premine — and no asterisks.

10-minute blocks

No premine

Any founder allocation is burned in public. The fair launch isn't a slogan — it's on-chain.

Proof-of-work

RandomX — CPU-optimized proof-of-work, the same algorithm that has secured Monero since 2019. No ASICs, no GPU farms: ordinary computers are the mining hardware.
