Why MoonBite

Don't trust us. Check it.

Most new coins sell you a story — a roadmap, a team, a chart going up. MoonBite hands you the receipts instead. Mine it yourself, hold your own keys, run your own node, and read every claim straight out of the code. Here's why it exists — and, just as honestly, what it isn't.

The case

Six things you can check right now.

Not slogans. Every one of these is something you can verify yourself before you trust a single word.

⚖️

A launch with no head start

No premine. No insider bag. No presale. Every MBITE is mined in the open under one published schedule — the founder starts at the same block height you do.

🔒

A cap that can't move

~33,000,000 MBITE, forever. A 50 MBITE reward that halves every 330,000 blocks. It lives in consensus code, not a promise — and it's unit-tested on regtest.

🔑

Nobody can freeze you

Your keys, your coins. The wallet is non-custodial — there's no MoonBite account, no custodian, and no one with a button that can freeze or claw back your balance.

🛰️

Run the whole stack yourself

Mine with the Reactor, verify with your own full node, read the chain in the open explorer. None of it depends on our servers staying honest.

🧱

Built on proven code

MoonBite is a fork of Litecoin Core, itself a Bitcoin Core descendant — over a decade of hardened, audited consensus code, with its proof-of-work swapped for RandomX — the CPU-optimized algorithm Monero has run since 2019.

🔎

We tell on ourselves

No fake volume. No invented listings. No price predictions. If something isn't live yet, we say so — right on this site. That discipline is the whole point.

The honest half

And here's what it isn't — yet.

A page that only bragged would be exactly the marketing we're rejecting. So here's the other side, no spin.

🚧

Not on mainnet

MoonBite is pre-mainnet — testnet and regtest only, today. No coin has market value, and there's nowhere to buy MBITE. It isn't listed on any exchange, full stop.

🏭

Not ASIC-resistant

RandomX is deliberately ASIC-resistant: the best mining hardware is an ordinary CPU, and that's by design. No warehouse of dedicated machines gets a structural edge over the laptop you already own. Monero has proven this holds up under real adversarial pressure since 2019.

🧪

Not audited, not an investment

This is experimental software with no production security audit. It's not a security, not financial advice, and not a promise of profit. Only play with what you can afford to lose.

🔭 Everything above is checkable. The tokenomics live in consensus code, the explorer shows the real chain, and the node guide lets you verify it yourself. If any claim here ever stops being true, it's a bug — tell us.
