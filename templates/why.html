Why MoonBite

Money you can actually verify.

Most new coins ask you to trust a story — a roadmap, a team, a chart. MoonBite asks you to trust nothing. Mine it yourself, hold your own keys, run your own node, and check every claim against the code. Here is why it exists, and — just as important — what it is not.

The case

Five reasons MoonBite exists

Not marketing lines — each of these is something you can check for yourself.

⚖️

A genuinely fair launch

No premine. No insider allocation. No presale. Every MBITE in existence is mined in the open under one published emission schedule — the same rules for the founder as for you.

🔒

A hard, honest cap

19,999,999.87 MBITE, ever. A 10 MBITE block reward halving every 1,000,000 blocks. Fixed in consensus code, not in a promise — and unit-tested on regtest.

🔑

Self-custody by default

Your keys, your coins. MoonBite ships a non-custodial wallet — there is no MoonBite account, no custodian, and nobody who can freeze or claw back your balance.

🛰️

You can run the whole thing

Mine with the Reactor, verify with your own full node, and read the chain with the open block explorer. Nothing here requires our servers to stay honest.

🧱

Built on proven code

MoonBite is a fork of Litecoin Core, itself a Bitcoin Core descendant — over a decade of hardened, audited consensus code, using the same scrypt proof-of-work Litecoin has secured since 2011.

🔎

Radical honesty

No fake volume. No invented exchange listings. No price predictions. If something isn't live yet, we say so — on this very site. That discipline is the whole point.

The honest half

What MoonBite is not — yet

A "why" page that only listed strengths would be exactly the kind of marketing we're rejecting. So here is the other side, plainly.

🚧

Not on mainnet

MoonBite is pre-mainnet. It runs on testnet and regtest today. No coin has market value, and you cannot buy MBITE anywhere — it isn't listed on any exchange.

🏭

Not ASIC-resistant

Scrypt is a mature algorithm with an established hardware ecosystem — CPUs, GPUs, and dedicated scrypt ASICs (the same class used for Litecoin) can all mine it. We won't pretend otherwise.

🧪

Not audited, not an investment

This is experimental software with no production security audit. It is not a security, not financial advice, and not a promise of profit. Only participate with what you can afford to lose.

🔭 Everything above is checkable. The tokenomics live in consensus code, the explorer shows the real chain, and the node guide lets you verify it yourself. If any claim here ever stops being true, it's a bug — tell us.
