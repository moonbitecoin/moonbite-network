What You Need to Know

Read this first.

MoonBite hands real financial sovereignty to individuals. That power comes with real responsibility, and there's no one to call if it goes wrong. Here's what that means, in plain language — no hype, no sugar-coating.

Learn the basics Getting started
MoonBite is pre-mainnet, open-source software. It is not an investment or a security. Coins mined today carry no market value. There is no exchange listing yet. Nothing on this site is financial or legal advice.
Status

Where MoonBite really stands

🚧
Pre-mainnet, not production
The testnet and regtest chains are live and fully working. Mainnet hasn't launched. The software is real, but the network is still being hardened before public launch. Expect rough edges.
💸
No market value today
MBITE is not listed on any exchange. You cannot buy or sell it for money today. Any coins you mine are real chain coins, but right now they're worth exactly nothing in dollars. Don't make financial decisions on a bet about some future price.
🧪
Experimental software
All cryptocurrency software — MoonBite's included — carries technical risk. Bugs, protocol changes, and surprises happen. The codebase is open source and auditable, but it isn't infallible.
🔮
No guaranteed future value
Nothing about MoonBite's design, capped supply, or roadmap guarantees MBITE will ever be worth money. A listing is on the roadmap, but a listing is not a price floor and not a promise of value.
Self-custody

You are the bank now

Self-custody means the responsibility stops with you. These aren't abstract risks — they're how people actually lose coins.

🔑
Lose your keys, lose your coins
Your private key (or seed phrase) is the only proof you own your MBITE. Lose it — a dead device, a mistaken delete, a misplaced piece of paper — and there is no recovery. Not from us, not from anyone. Write your seed phrase on paper, keep it somewhere safe, and back it up in at least two places.
↩️
Transactions are irreversible
Once a transaction confirms on the chain, it can't be reversed, recalled, or disputed. Send MBITE to the wrong address, or get tricked into sending it, and the coins are gone. Check the full address before you broadcast — glancing at the first and last few characters is not enough.
🤫
Never share your seed phrase
No real MoonBite service — not this site, not the wallet, the miner, or the explorer — will ever ask for your seed phrase or private key. Anyone who asks is running a scam. Full stop.
🧾
Backups are on you
There's no MoonBite support team that can restore your wallet. Backing up your seed phrase isn't a one-time chore — it's an ongoing job. A single sheet of paper in one spot is one fire, flood, or theft away from gone.
Legal & taxes

Your country, your rules

⚖️

Taxes vary by country

In many places, mining crypto is a taxable event, and spending or exchanging it can trigger more tax on top. MoonBite gives no tax advice. Talk to a qualified tax professional in your country before you mine or transact MBITE.

🌍

Legal status varies by country

Crypto is regulated differently — and in some places banned outright — depending on where you are. Knowing and following the law where you live is on you. MoonBite provides the tools; how you use them is your call.

📋

Not financial advice

Nothing on this site, in the wallet, the miner, or the explorer is financial advice. MoonBite is a technology project. Whether and how you use it is entirely your decision.

Stay safe

How to protect yourself

The things that actually burn people are social engineering, fake software, and careless backups — not protocol bugs.

⬇️
Download only from official sources
The wallet, miner, and node software come from this site and the official source repository — nowhere else. Don't install MoonBite software from third-party sites, social media links, or messages you didn't ask for.
🎭
Impersonators are everywhere
Scammers routinely pose as crypto projects, their developers, and their social accounts. MoonBite will never DM you to offer help, hand out "free MBITE," or ask you to send coins to a wallet.
✅
Check addresses character by character
Some malware ("clipboard hijackers") swaps a copied address for an attacker's the moment you paste. Before you send, confirm several characters at the start and end of the address match what you meant to use.
📡
Use your own node for big checks
A block explorer you don't control could, in theory, show you false data. For any high-value transaction, confirm it through your own node's RPC. The chain doesn't lie.
📵
Keep seed phrases offline
A seed phrase in a phone note, email draft, or cloud doc is one data breach away from stolen. Paper, a stamped metal plate, or other offline media, stored somewhere safe, is the standard.
🔗
Know what a scam looks like
The classics: "double your MBITE" giveaways, fake support staff fishing for seed phrases, and phishing sites dressed up as moonbite.org. Read the scams guide before you deal with anyone claiming to be MoonBite.
