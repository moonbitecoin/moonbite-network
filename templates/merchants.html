How it works

Nobody holds the money but you

🧾

1 · Raise a request

You type an amount. We make a payment link that points at your address. No account, no deposit, no custody.

📲

2 · Customer pays you

They send MBITE from their wallet straight to your address. The coins never pass through us.

👀

3 · We confirm on-chain

The server watches the chain and flips the request to paid the moment your address gets the coins. Watch-only. No keys, ever.

🔎 Demo checks run against this educational node. A payment is detected when your address actually receives coins here; in production the same check points at the MoonBite node. Prices are set in MBITE — a fiat peg needs real trades, and those don't exist yet.
MoonBite is a pre-mainnet, open-source project. The merchant tools are non-custodial software: the server stores directory listings and payment requests, nothing more. It never holds coins, keys, or balances. Nothing here is financial advice or an offer to sell a security.
