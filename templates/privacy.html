Privacy

Your privacy, plainly stated.

No trackers. No ads. No analytics beacons. Here is exactly what this site does and does not collect — in plain English, not legalese. This is a summary, not a formal legal document.

Short version: this site collects nothing unless you voluntarily submit your email address in the notify form. We never sell, share, or market to you. See details below.
Data collection

What we collect and why

If you submit the "notify me" form on this site, we store your email address. That is the only personal data we collect. Submitting is entirely voluntary — nothing on this site requires it.

Where it is stored: On the server running this website, in a plain text file (notify_signups.jsonl). It is not sent to a third-party email platform, a CRM, or any external service.

Why we collect it: One reason only — to notify you when MBITE is listed on an exchange. We will send one email when that happens. We will not email you before then, we will not add you to a newsletter, and we will not send marketing communications.

How to be removed: Contact us via the GitHub repository with the email address you submitted and we will delete it from the file.

This site does not load Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Hotjar, Mixpanel, or any other third-party tracking or analytics script. We do not run ads. We do not embed social media widgets that phone home.

We do not collect IP addresses, browser fingerprints, referrer URLs, or behavioral data. We have no interest in tracking you across the web.

Like all web servers, our hosting environment may produce standard server logs (request timestamps, HTTP status codes, rough visitor counts) for operational purposes such as detecting errors or attacks. These logs are not used for advertising, profiling, or identification of individual users, and are not shared with third parties.

Your browser

Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising cookies or session-tracking cookies. Here is what the site does store in your browser and why.

🎨

Theme preference

If you switch between light and dark mode, we store your preference in localStorage under the key theme. This is a convenience setting that stays on your device and is never transmitted to the server.

📧

Notify email fallback

When you submit the notify form, the site's JavaScript also saves your email in localStorage as a client-side fallback, in case the server request fails. This data never leaves your device unless the form submission succeeds.

🍪

No tracking cookies

We do not set advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies, or session identifiers tied to your identity. If your browser reports a cookie from this site, it is a functional necessity — not a tracker.

Your wallet

We never see your keys or balances

🔑

Self-custody by design

MoonBite is a self-custodial system. Your private keys and seed phrase are generated on your device and remain there. This website and its server have no access to your wallet, your seed phrase, your private keys, or your coin balance — and never will.

📡

What the node sees

When you broadcast a transaction or query an address, the MoonBite node processes that request. Like all public blockchains, transactions are visible to anyone running a node. We do not log queries beyond normal server operation, and we do not link queries to identities.

Sharing
