Press & Brand

Press & brand.

Writing about MoonBite? Here is the accurate boilerplate, brand basics, fast facts, and how to reach the project. Please use the copy below verbatim where possible — we want coverage to be correct.

MoonBite is pre-mainnet and MBITE is not trading on any exchange. Please do not report a price, market cap, or trading volume — none exists. Do not quote funding amounts, venture backing, or partnership deals — there are none.
Boilerplate

Official one-paragraph description

Copy this exactly for news articles, listings, and directories.

"MoonBite (ticker: MBITE) is a pre-mainnet, open-source cryptocurrency built on a Litecoin/Bitcoin-lineage codebase. It uses scrypt proof-of-work, has a hard maximum supply of 19,999,999.87 MBITE, and launched with no premine and no presale — every coin is mined under one public emission schedule. The project is self-custodial by design: users hold their own private keys. MoonBite is currently available on testnet and regtest; mainnet launch and exchange listing are forthcoming. MBITE is not an investment or a security and carries no promise of future value."

Brand basics

Name, mark, and usage

🌙
Name & wordmark

The project name is MoonBite — one word, capital M, capital B. The wordmark is 🌙 MoonBite with the crescent moon glyph. The ticker is MBITE — all caps.

Avoid: "Moonbite," "Moon Bite," "Moon-Bite," "$MBITE" (before listing), or "MoonBite Coin" as a formal name.

The moon mark

The favicon/mark is a crescent moon SVG — the same icon used in the browser tab on this site. It is the primary graphic symbol of the project.

When referencing the mark in digital contexts, you can link to the SVG at /static/favicon.svg on this site. Do not alter the mark's shape, add drop shadows, or recolor it outside of the original palette.

🎨
Accent color

MoonBite's accent is a warm gold-amber gradient used for emphasis. It represents the moon's glow. Avoid using generic blue or green crypto palettes when representing the MoonBite brand — these colors are not associated with the project.

✍️
Tone of voice

Sober, honest, and direct. MoonBite does not use hyperbolic price language, moon/lambo memes, or "to the moon" rhetoric in official materials. When covering the project, accurate and measured language is preferred.

Contact

How to reach the project

MoonBite is an open-source project with no PR department or marketing team. Here is how to get in touch accurately.

💻

GitHub — preferred

The primary point of contact is the GitHub repository: github.com/moonbitecoin/MoonBite-Coin. Open an issue or a discussion thread for press inquiries, corrections, or factual questions. This ensures transparency — others can see the exchange.

🚫

No press office, no spokesperson

There is no dedicated press email, no communications team, and no authorized spokesperson beyond the repository maintainers. Any individual claiming to speak for MoonBite through unofficial channels should be verified against the GitHub account.
