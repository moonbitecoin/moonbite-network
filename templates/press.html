Press & Brand

Get it right.

Writing about MoonBite? Here's the accurate boilerplate, the brand basics, the fast facts, and how to reach the project. Use the copy below verbatim where you can — we'd rather the coverage be correct than flattering.

MoonBite is pre-mainnet and MBITE trades on no exchange. Please don't report a price, market cap, or trading volume — none exists. Don't quote funding rounds, venture backing, or partnerships either. There are none.
Boilerplate

Official one-paragraph description

Copy this word for word into articles, listings, and directories.

"MoonBite (ticker: MBITE) is a pre-mainnet, open-source cryptocurrency built on a Litecoin/Bitcoin-lineage codebase. It uses RandomX proof-of-work (CPU-optimized, ASIC-resistant), has a hard maximum supply of ~33,000,000 MBITE, and launched with no premine and no presale — every coin is mined under one public emission schedule. The project is self-custodial by design: users hold their own private keys. MoonBite is currently available on testnet and regtest; mainnet launch and exchange listing are forthcoming. MBITE is not an investment or a security and carries no promise of future value."
Fast facts

The numbers, straight

🔡

Ticker

MBITE

🧮

Maximum supply

~33,000,000 MBITE. A hard cap, enforced in consensus code.

⏱️

Block time

10 minutes (150-second target).

⚙️

Proof-of-work algorithm

RandomX. The CPU-optimized, ASIC-resistant algorithm that has secured Monero since 2019.

📉

Halving schedule

The block reward halves every 330,000 blocks (~6.27 years). It starts at 50 MBITE per block.

🚀

Launch status

Pre-mainnet. Testnet and regtest are running. No mainnet date is set yet.

⚖️

Premine

None. No founder allocation, no presale, no airdrop. A fair launch, full stop.

💻

Codebase lineage

A Litecoin Core fork, itself a Bitcoin Core descendant. Open-source.

🔑

Custody model

Self-custodial. No accounts. No company holds user funds.

Brand basics

Name, mark, and usage

🌙
Name & wordmark

The name is MoonBite — one word, capital M, capital B. The wordmark is 🌙 MoonBite with the crescent glyph. The ticker is MBITE, all caps.

Don't write: "Moonbite," "Moon Bite," "Moon-Bite," "$MBITE" (not before listing), or "MoonBite Coin" as a formal name.

The moon mark

The mark is a crescent moon SVG — the same icon in this site's browser tab. It's the project's primary graphic symbol.

Need it digitally? Link to the SVG at /static/favicon.svg. Don't reshape the mark, don't add drop shadows, and don't recolor it outside the original palette.

🎨
Accent color

The accent is a warm gold-amber gradient used for emphasis. It stands for the moon's glow. Skip the generic blue and green crypto palettes when you represent MoonBite — those colors aren't ours.

✍️
Tone of voice

Sober, honest, direct. No hype-price talk, no moon/lambo memes, no "to the moon" in official materials. When you cover the project, measured and accurate beats loud every time.

Contact

How to reach the project

MoonBite has no PR department and no marketing team. Here's how to actually get in touch.

💻

GitHub, first choice

The main point of contact is the repository: github.com/moonbitecoin/MoonBite-Coin. Open an issue or a discussion for press questions, corrections, or fact checks. It's public, so everyone can see the answer too.

🚫

No press office, no spokesperson

There's no press email, no comms team, and no authorized spokesperson beyond the repository maintainers. Anyone claiming to speak for MoonBite through some other channel should be checked against the GitHub account first.
