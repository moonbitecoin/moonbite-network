What changes at listing

What a listing actually means

A listing makes MBITE tradable. It doesn't hand it a value, and it doesn't turn it into an investment. Here's the honest difference.

📊

Price discovery begins

For the first time, buyers and sellers hash out a price in an open order book. We don't set it. Nobody sets it. The market does.

🔄

Trading becomes possible

Miners who want to swap rewards for another currency get a venue. People who want MBITE but don't mine finally get a way in.

🔑

Self-custody still matters

An exchange holds coins for you — which means it can lose them for you. Keep your MBITE in a wallet you control and exchange risk never touches you, before or after any listing.
