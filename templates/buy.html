What changes at listing

What a listing actually means

A listing makes MBITE tradable — it does not assign it an intrinsic value or make it an investment.

📊

Price discovery begins

For the first time, buyers and sellers will agree on prices in an open order book. Neither this project nor anyone else sets that price.

🔄

Trading becomes possible

Miners who want to convert rewards to another currency will have a venue. People who want MBITE but don't mine will have a way to acquire it.

🔑

Self-custody still matters

An exchange holds coins on your behalf. Keeping your MBITE in a self-custody wallet means you're never exposed to exchange risk — before or after any listing.
