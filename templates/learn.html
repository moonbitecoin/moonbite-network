FAQ

Frequently asked questions

MoonBite is an open, fair-launch cryptocurrency built on a Litecoin-lineage core. It's proof-of-work money you mine on your own hardware, hold in your own wallet, and verify with your own node. No premine, and a fixed maximum supply of ~33,000,000 MBITE.
Not fully. MoonBite is pre-mainnet. You can run it on testnet and regtest today, mine it, and explore the chain — but coins carry no market value yet, and it isn't trading anywhere.
No. MBITE isn't listed on any exchange, so there's no buy button. The only way to get it right now is to mine it. Listings are on the roadmap — get notified when one goes live.
Make an address in the wallet, then run a miner — browser, one-click desktop, or command line. The block reward pays straight to your address. Full walkthrough in the mining guide.
Every hash your miner computes is an independent lottery ticket. On a live network your chance of finding any given block is just your share of the total hashrate — if you run 1% of the network's power, you find roughly 1 block in every 100, and your expected time per block is the block time (2.5 min) divided by that share. Faster hardware buys a bigger share, nothing more. One honest catch: mining is memoryless. A long dry spell doesn't make you "due" — after a million misses the very next hash still wins with the exact same tiny probability. There's no streak, no momentum, no posterior that shifts in your favour from past failures.
You hold your own private keys instead of trusting a company to hold them for you. The MoonBite wallet is non-custodial: your keys never leave your device, and this site will never ask for your seed phrase or private key.
No. MoonBite is an open-source technology project, not an investment or a security. Nothing here is financial advice, and nobody is promising future value.
Glossary

Words worth knowing

⛓️

Block

A batch of transactions added to the chain. New blocks are found through mining.

⛏️

Proof-of-work

The mining process that secures the network by requiring real computational work.

👛

Wallet

Software that holds your keys and lets you send and receive MBITE.

🖧

Node

Software that validates and relays the chain, enforcing the rules independently.

📉

Halving

A scheduled cut to the block reward — MoonBite halves every 330,000 blocks.

🧾

Mempool

The waiting room of unconfirmed transactions before they're mined into a block.
