FAQ

Frequently asked questions

MoonBite is an open, fair-launch cryptocurrency built on a Litecoin-lineage core. It's proof-of-work money you can mine on your own hardware, hold in a self-custody wallet, and verify with your own node. There is no premine and a fixed maximum supply of 19,999,999.87 MBITE.
MoonBite is pre-mainnet. You can run it on testnet and regtest today, mine it, and explore the chain — but coins carry no market value yet and it is not trading anywhere.
Not yet — MBITE is not listed on any exchange. The only way to get it right now is to mine it. Exchange listings are on the roadmap; get notified when one goes live.
Create an address in the wallet, then run one of the miners — a browser miner, a one-click desktop miner, or the command-line miner. The block reward is paid to your address. See the mining guide.
You hold your own private keys instead of trusting a company to hold them for you. The MoonBite wallet is non-custodial: your keys never leave your device, and this site will never ask you for a seed phrase or private key.
No. MoonBite is an open-source technology project, not an investment or a security. Nothing here is financial advice, and there is no promise of future value.
Glossary

Words worth knowing

⛓️

Block

A batch of transactions added to the chain. New blocks are found through mining.

⛏️

Proof-of-work

The mining process that secures the network by requiring real computational work.

👛

Wallet

Software that holds your keys and lets you send and receive MBITE.

🖧

Node

Software that validates and relays the chain, enforcing the rules independently.

📉

Halving

A scheduled cut to the block reward — MoonBite halves every 1,000,000 blocks.

🧾

Mempool

The waiting room of unconfirmed transactions before they're mined into a block.
