Self-custody

You hold the keys. Nobody else. Not even us.

"Be your own bank" isn't a slogan here — it's the architecture. Four things it means the second you press generate.

🔑

Your key never leaves the room

The private key is generated on your device and never transmitted. MoonBite's servers — and this website — never see it. The flip side is real: lose your seed phrase and nobody can bring your coins back. That responsibility is the price of actually owning something.

🚫

Nothing to freeze, nothing to close

There's no account to get locked out of. A moon1… address is all it takes to receive MBITE, and no one can block, reverse, or seize a confirmed transaction on the chain.

🔗

Addresses start with moon1…

Mainnet uses bech32 addresses prefixed moon1 (testnet tmoon1). Readable, easy to copy-paste, with a built-in checksum that catches typos before you ever hit send.

🌐

The network answers to no one

Every rule is enforced by nodes, not by us. Run your own and you confirm every transaction yourself. No company sits in the middle waiting to be trusted — the network is its own authority.

Straight talk: MoonBite is pre-mainnet. Coins you mine today carry no market value, and MBITE is listed on no exchange. This is not an investment or a security. Mine to own a piece of the network — not because you're counting on a payout.
