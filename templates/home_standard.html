How it works

Three rules. No committee.

Mined, not minted

A computer guesses until it finds a valid block. The network pays it the block reward. That is the only way a coin comes to exist.

Blocks every {{ consensus.block_time_min }} minutes

Difficulty adjusts every 60 blocks so the spacing holds whether ten machines are mining or ten thousand.

A fixed ceiling

What is not true yet

Read this before you mine.

There is no price.MBITE is not listed anywhere. Nobody can sell you one, and anyone offering to is lying.
It may never be worth anything.Mining consumes electricity that may never be recovered.
There is no team allocation.No presale, no airdrop, no early access. Every coin is mined.
Nobody can help you recover a wallet.Your keys are yours alone. Lose them and the coins are gone, by design.

MBITE is a proof-of-work cryptocurrency. It is not an investment, a security, or a promise of future value. It has no guaranteed price, no listing, and no team allocation. Mining consumes electricity that may never be recovered.
