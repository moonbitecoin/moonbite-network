In plain words

No bank did that. Nobody did. You earned it.

Picture a giant notebook the whole world shares, that nobody can secretly change. To add a line — "this piece belongs to you" — a computer has to solve a hard little puzzle first. When you bit the moon, yours did. The network handed it a coin for the work, exactly like it does for everyone. That's all money has ever been: a record people agree on. MoonBite just doesn't need anyone in charge of the notebook.
