What is MoonBite?

MoonBite is a Litecoin fork designed for absolute fairness. It has no premine, no ICO, no venture capital, and no founders' allocation. The first block was mined on the same terms as every block that follows: 50 MBITE to whoever secures the network.

In a space where nearly every cryptocurrency project involves some form of founder advantage—whether through mining rewards reserved in advance, token allocation, or exchange listings that benefit insiders—MoonBite offers something simpler: a network where everyone has the same opportunity from day one.

You don't buy MBITE. You mine it. You don't trust the founders. You trust mathematics.

Why Mine MoonBite?

  • Your coins go directly to your wallet.

    No account, no intermediary, no middleman. MBITE arrives in the address you control, on the blockchain you can verify. You own your wealth.

  • You're not gambling on an exchange listing.

    The value of MBITE emerges naturally from supply and demand. It's not dependent on venture capitalists deciding when to sell or a marketing team hyping adoption.

  • Mining is proof of early participation.

    Your mining receipt shows the block you secured, the timestamp, the block hash. It's verifiable. It's real. You can share #MinedMyFirstBlock knowing it's cryptographic fact, not speculation.

  • You're contributing to network security.

    Every block you mine makes MoonBite stronger and more decentralized. The network's consensus depends on the miners who run it.

  • The math doesn't change.

    50 MBITE per block. Difficulty auto-adjusts every 2016 blocks. Halving every 700,000 blocks. These rules are immutable. Founders can't dilute the supply. The network can't be stopped.

Why Fair Launch Matters

The crypto industry was built on a promise: decentralization, no middleman, no central authority. But most projects betray that promise from day one with a premine, founder allocation, or ICO that benefits insiders.

MoonBite takes the promise seriously. If decentralization is the point, then fairness at launch is non-negotiable. Everyone starts at block zero. The math is transparent. The network can't be gamed.
