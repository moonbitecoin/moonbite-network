How settlement works

You trade wallet-to-wallet

The order book is just a meeting point. When two people agree, they swap coins directly — no middleman ever holds the money.

📝

1 · Post or take an offer

Add a buy or sell order, or find one you like in the book. You share only the addresses where each of you gets paid.

🔒

2 · Atomic swap

Both sides lock funds in a hash-time-locked contract. Either the whole trade happens, or everyone gets their coins back. Nobody can run off with the money.

✅

3 · Done

MBITE ⇄ LTC or BTC swaps natively (shared Bitcoin script family). USDT needs a smart-contract leg. The server just recorded that you two matched.

🚧 Automated swap coordination isn't built yet (Phase 2). For now the exchange matches orders and hands you both the addresses; you run the atomic swap with an external tool. We will never route funds through this server.
