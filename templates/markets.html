How settlement works

No middleman ever holds the money.

The order book is just where two people shake hands. When you agree, the coins move directly between your wallets — the math guarantees it, not us.

📝

1 · Post or take an offer

Add a buy or sell order, or grab one you like from the book. The only thing you share is the address where you get paid.

🔒

2 · Atomic swap

Both sides lock funds in a hash-time-locked contract. Either the full trade clears, or everyone gets their coins back. There is no version where one side walks away with both.

✅

3 · Done

MBITE ⇄ LTC or BTC swaps natively — they share the Bitcoin script family. USDT needs a smart-contract leg. All the server ever did was note that you two matched.

🚧 Automated swap coordination isn't built yet (Phase 2). Today the exchange matches orders and hands you both the addresses; you run the atomic swap with an external tool. Funds will never route through this server — not now, not after Phase 2.
