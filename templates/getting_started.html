Getting Started

Zero to your first block.

No premine. Every MBITE that exists was mined by someone. These four steps take you from a blank slate to a confirmed balance on the chain.

Step by step

Your first four moves

Each step hands you the right tool. Go as deep as you want at every stage.

Step 1

Get a wallet

Download and install the MoonBite wallet. It generates your private key locally — that key never leaves your device. Setup hands you a seed phrase: write it on paper, lock it somewhere safe, and never hand it to anyone. Not a support agent, not this website, not anyone.

Download wallet →
Step 2

Generate a receive address

Inside the wallet, spin up a new receive address. Mainnet addresses start with moon1; testnet with tmoon1. This is where your mining rewards land. Copy it — the next step needs it.

Open wallet →
Step 3

Mine your first MBITE

Open the miner and paste in your address. It runs RandomX proof-of-work to compete for block rewards. Find a valid block and the whole reward pays straight to your address — no pool, no middleman, no cut. Testnet rewards are up for grabs right now.

Start mining →
Step 4

Verify it in the explorer

Paste your address into the block explorer and watch the balance climb in real time. Every confirmation is written on-chain. No account, no login, nobody's permission needed to check what you own — the chain is the record.

Open explorer →
What to expect

The honest version for new miners

⏱️

Block time: ~10 minutes

The network aims for a new block every 600 seconds. Your odds of finding one track your share of the total hash rate. On testnet, where competition is thin, blocks come fast.

🎁

Current reward: 50 MBITE

The genesis block subsidy is 50 MBITE, and it halves every 330,000 blocks. Total supply is capped at ~33,000,000 MBITE — for all time, no exceptions.

💰

No market value yet

MBITE is pre-mainnet and listed on nothing. The coins you mine now are real chain coins, but they carry no monetary value today. Mine to take part — not because you're expecting a price.

🔑

Seed phrase = your coins

Lose the seed phrase and the coins are gone for good. No support team can bring them back — that team doesn't exist. Write it down, keep it offline, back it up.

⛏️

RandomX PoW

MoonBite runs on RandomX proof-of-work — the CPU-optimized algorithm behind Monero. A plain CPU is the ideal miner: GPUs gain almost nothing and no ASIC exists. The laptop you already own is competitive hardware.

🌐

Run a node to go deeper

A full node validates the whole chain on your own machine. Optional for individuals — but it cuts your dependence on third-party servers to zero.

Quick reference

Terminal, if that's your thing

Prefer the command line? These snippets get you moving on testnet.

# Check your wallet balance on testnet
./moonbite-cli -testnet getbalance

# Generate a new receive address
./moonbite-cli -testnet getnewaddress

# Start the daemon and begin syncing
./moonbited -testnet -daemon

# Check how many blocks you've synced
./moonbite-cli -testnet getblockcount
