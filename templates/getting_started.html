Getting Started

From zero to first block.

No premine means every MBITE in existence was mined by someone. These four steps walk you from a blank slate to a confirmed balance in the chain.

Get the wallet Learn the basics
Step by step

Your first four actions

Each step links to the right tool. You can go as deep as you like at each stage.

Step 1

Get a wallet

Download and install the MoonBite wallet. Your wallet generates a private key locally — it never leaves your device. During setup you'll receive a seed phrase: write it down on paper, keep it somewhere safe, and never share it with anyone including this website.

Download wallet →
Step 2

Generate a receive address

Inside the wallet, generate a new receive address. Mainnet addresses start with moon1; testnet addresses start with tmoon1. This is where your mining rewards will land. Copy the address — you'll need it in the next step.

Open wallet →
Step 3

Mine your first MBITE

Open the miner and paste in your wallet address. The miner uses scrypt proof-of-work to compete for block rewards. When your miner finds a valid block, the full reward is paid directly to your address — no pool, no middleman. Testnet rewards are available to claim right now.

Start mining →
Step 4

Verify it in the explorer

Paste your address into the block explorer and watch the balance update in real time. Each new block confirmation is recorded on-chain. You don't need an account, a login, or anyone's permission to verify your own balance — the chain is the record.

Open explorer →
What to expect

Honest expectations for new miners

⏱️

Block time: ~2.5 minutes

The network targets a new block every 150 seconds. Your odds of finding a block depend on your share of the total network hash rate. On testnet with low competition, blocks come quickly.

🎁

Current reward: 10 MBITE

The genesis block reward is 10 MBITE. It halves every 1,000,000 blocks. The total supply is capped at 19,999,999.87 MBITE across all time.

💰

No market value yet

MBITE is pre-mainnet and not listed on any exchange. Coins you mine now are real chain coins, but they carry no monetary value today. Mine because you want to participate, not because you expect a price.

🔑

Seed phrase = your coins

Lose the seed phrase and the coins are gone — permanently. There is no support team that can recover them. Write it down, keep it offline, and back it up.

⛏️

Scrypt PoW

MoonBite uses the scrypt proof-of-work algorithm. A standard CPU or GPU can participate. Dedicated scrypt ASICs (Litecoin miners) will also work on mainnet.

🌐

Run a node to go deeper

Running a full node lets you validate the entire chain locally. It's optional for individuals but gives you complete independence from third-party servers.

Quick reference

Useful terminal commands

If you prefer the command line, these snippets get you started on testnet.

# Check your wallet balance on testnet
./moonbite-cli -testnet getbalance

# Generate a new receive address
./moonbite-cli -testnet getnewaddress

# Start the daemon and begin syncing
./moonbited -testnet -daemon

# Check how many blocks you've synced
./moonbite-cli -testnet getblockcount
