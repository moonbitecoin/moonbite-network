The honest situation

What selling actually requires

A sell venue needs two things that don't exist yet: an exchange listing and a willing buyer with a public price. Neither is in place.

🔑

You already control your coins

If you mined MBITE, the reward landed in your self-custody wallet. You hold the private keys — not this site, not a custodian. Your coins cannot be frozen or seized by a third party. That is the state they're in right now, and it's the safest state to be in while there's no exchange.

🕐

A listing is what unlocks trading

When an exchange lists MBITE, sellers can post orders and buyers can fill them. Until that happens there's no public market mechanism and therefore no sell price. The roadmap covers this honestly — no date has been committed.

Self-custody matters

Why holding your own keys is the right call now

During a pre-listing period there are no custodians to trust and no need to trust any. Here's what self-custody actually gives you.

🛡️

No exchange risk

Your MBITE lives in your wallet. No exchange insolvency, freeze, or hack can touch coins you hold yourself.

⏳

No deadline to act

Self-custody doesn't expire. You can hold as long as you like, wait for a listing, and decide then — no pressure.

🔎

Verify your balance yourself

Run your own node or use the block explorer to confirm your address balance. No one else's word required.

📤

Send to any MBITE address

Even without an exchange you can send MBITE peer-to-peer to another MBITE address. That's been possible from day one.

🔐

Timelock vault

The node includes a timelock vault: lock coins until a future block, then spend normally. A useful tool for miners who want to keep coins safe for a set period.

🌐

Part of the network

Every wallet that holds MBITE and every address in the UTXO set is part of the ledger. You're not on a waiting list — you're already participating.

Internal order book

Peer-to-peer intent matching

There is a non-custodial internal order book where you can post a sell intent and let a willing buyer find it. Settlement is an atomic wallet-to-wallet swap — the server never holds coins. This is experimental pre-listing infrastructure, not a guarantee of liquidity.

📝

Post a sell intent

Name a price and amount in the internal order book. If someone matches your offer, you both run an atomic swap wallet-to-wallet. The server stores only the public intent — no coins ever pass through it.

Open the order book →
⚠️

What this isn't

The internal exchange is experimental. Automated swap coordination isn't built yet, so settlement requires both parties to run the swap manually. It is not a liquid market and not a guarantee that your order will fill.
