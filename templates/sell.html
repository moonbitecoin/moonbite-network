The honest situation

What a sale actually takes

Selling needs two things that don't exist yet: a place to list your coins, and a buyer willing to pay a public price. Neither is here. So we're straight with you about it.

🔑

You already hold your coins

If you mined MBITE, the reward landed in your own wallet. You hold the keys — not this site, not a custodian. No third party can freeze or seize them. That's exactly where they are right now, and it's the safest place to be while there's no exchange.

🕐

A listing is what starts trading

When an exchange lists MBITE, sellers post orders and buyers fill them. Until that day there's no public market and no sell price. The roadmap says so plainly — and no date has been promised.

Self-custody matters

Holding your own keys is the right call now

With no exchange in the picture, there's no custodian to trust — and no need for one. Here's what self-custody actually buys you.

🛡️

No exchange risk

Your MBITE lives in your wallet. No exchange blowup, freeze, or hack can touch coins you hold yourself.

⏳

No clock ticking

Self-custody doesn't expire. Hold as long as you like, wait for a listing, and decide then. Nobody's rushing you.

🔎

Check your balance yourself

Run your own node or use the block explorer to confirm your balance. You don't have to take anyone's word for it.

📤

Send to any MBITE address

No exchange needed to move coins. Send MBITE peer-to-peer to any other address. That's worked from day one.

🔐

Timelock vault

The node ships with a timelock vault: lock coins until a future block, then spend them normally. Handy for miners who want to sit on coins for a set stretch.

🌐

Already in the network

Every wallet that holds MBITE, every address in the UTXO set, is part of the ledger. You're not on a waiting list. You're already in.

Internal order book

Post an offer. Wait for a match.

There's a non-custodial internal order book where you can post a sell offer and let a willing buyer find it. Settlement is an atomic wallet-to-wallet swap — the server never touches your coins. This is experimental pre-listing plumbing, not a promise that anyone will buy.

📝

Post a sell offer

Name your price and amount on the internal order book. If someone takes it, you both run an atomic swap, wallet to wallet. The server only stores the public offer — no coins ever pass through it.

Open the order book →
⚠️

What this isn't

The internal exchange is experimental. Automated swap coordination isn't built yet, so both parties settle the swap by hand. It is not a liquid market, and there's no guarantee your offer ever gets filled.
