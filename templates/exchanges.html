What listing means

What changes when MBITE lists

An exchange listing is a milestone, not a guarantee of value. Here's what it actually means.

📋

Public order book

Buyers and sellers post prices openly. The market sets a price — nobody decides it in advance.

🔓

Liquidity for miners

Miners who have earned MBITE on testnet (and later mainnet) can choose to offer coins for trade if they wish.

🚫

Not a price promise

A listing creates a market. It does not guarantee any particular price, volume, or return. Speculate at your own risk.

Internal swap

Testnet atomic swaps today

While we wait for mainnet and external listings, the internal markets tool lets you post peer-to-peer swap offers on testnet. The server is non-custodial — it never holds your coins or keys.

🔄

Non-custodial order book

Post a buy or sell offer against LTC, BTC, or USDT. The server matches intents; the actual swap settles wallet-to-wallet via an atomic contract.

How it works
