What listing means

What a listing actually changes

A listing is a milestone, not a payday. Here's what it does — and what it doesn't.

📋

Public order book

Buyers and sellers post prices in the open. The market decides the number — nobody sets it in advance, not even us.

🔓

Liquidity for miners

Miners who earned MBITE on testnet — and later mainnet — get the option to trade their coins. Nobody forces them to.

🚫

Not a price promise

A listing opens a market. It guarantees no price, no volume, no return. If you speculate, that's on you.

Internal swap

Atomic swaps, on testnet, today

Mainnet and real listings can wait. Right now the internal markets tool lets you post peer-to-peer swap offers on testnet. The server never touches your coins or keys — it can't.

🔄

Non-custodial order book

Post a buy or sell offer against LTC, BTC, or USDT. The server just matches intent. The swap itself settles wallet-to-wallet through an atomic contract.
