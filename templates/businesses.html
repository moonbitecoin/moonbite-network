Why now

Built into the protocol, not bolted on

These aren't features we sell you. They're consequences of how the chain is designed — and they change how your business holds and moves money.

⚡
10-minute blocks
MoonBite targets a new block every 600 seconds, matching Bitcoin's target. Less waiting at the counter, faster confirmation before you hand over the goods.
🔐
Your treasury, your keys
Hold MBITE in a wallet you control. No exchange account, no custodian sitting between you and your money, no counterparty to go under. Your keys, your balance.
📋
Address-per-order invoicing
Spin up a unique bech32 address for every order and watch it on-chain. You don't need an API key to get paid — just a wallet and a block explorer.
🖧
Verify it yourself
A local node checks every payment against the full chain history. You confirm receipts yourself instead of trusting someone else's API to tell you the truth.
📉
No surprises in the supply
Supply is capped at ~33,000,000 MBITE, the halving schedule is public, and there was no premine. Nobody can inflate your holdings away with a hidden founder stash.
🌐
Open source, top to bottom
Every piece of the stack — node, wallet, miner, explorer — is open source. Read the code, run it yourself, fork it if you need something we don't ship.
