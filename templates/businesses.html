Why now

Benefits built into the protocol

MoonBite's design choices directly affect how a business can hold and move money.

⚡
2.5-minute confirmations
MoonBite targets a new block every 150 seconds — roughly twice as fast as Bitcoin. Faster confirmation times mean less customer wait at the point of acceptance.
🔐
Non-custodial treasury
Hold MBITE in a wallet you control. No exchange account needed, no counterparty risk from a third-party custodian. Your keys, your balance.
📋
Address-based invoicing
Generate a unique bech32 address per order and monitor it on-chain. No API key required to receive funds — just a wallet and a block explorer.
🖧
Run your own node
A local MoonBite node confirms transactions against the full chain history. You verify every payment yourself instead of trusting a third-party API.
📉
Predictable emission
The supply is capped, the halving schedule is public, and there was no premine. No sudden inflation from a hidden founder allocation.
🌐
Open-source infrastructure
Every piece of MoonBite's stack — node, wallet, miner, explorer — is open source. Audit the code, run it yourself, fork it if you need to.
