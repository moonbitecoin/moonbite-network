  Fix: Wait 5-10 seconds for node to fully start

  Fix: This is NORMAL! Difficulty increases each retarget, slowing mining

Issue: Test hangs at a retarget
  Fix: Give it more time (can take 1-2 hours per retarget once hard)

Issue: Difficulty goes down (oscillates)
  Fix: This should NOT happen! Check that pow.py was properly updated
